# The Scholar Cat Cabinet

22 one-button arcade games with the same cat in every one. Space or tap is the only control; each
game asks the button a different question.

**Play them at [martindavinci.github.io/mdv-demo-arcade](https://martindavinci.github.io/mdv-demo-arcade/)**

## The games

**When to press** — a moment, and you lose for being early or late.

- [Colonnade](https://martindavinci.github.io/mdv-demo-arcade/games/colonnade.html) — a winged cat between marble gates at twilight. *Tap to flap.*
- [Catwalk](https://martindavinci.github.io/mdv-demo-arcade/games/catwalk.html) — an isometric ledge that turns without warning. *Tap to turn.*
- [Undercroft](https://martindavinci.github.io/mdv-demo-arcade/games/undercroft.html) — run the vaults, floor and ceiling both. *Tap to flip gravity.*
- [Belfry](https://martindavinci.github.io/mdv-demo-arcade/games/belfry.html) — climb a bell tower wall to wall. *Tap to leap.*
- [Orrery](https://martindavinci.github.io/mdv-demo-arcade/games/orrery.html) — swing between brass spheres in the dark. *Tap to let go.*
- [Compline](https://martindavinci.github.io/mdv-demo-arcade/games/compline.html) — wait by candlelight for something to land. *Strike, or don't.*
- [Escapement](https://martindavinci.github.io/mdv-demo-arcade/games/escapement.html) — keep a clock alive with your own timing. *Tap on each pass.*
- [Tablature](https://martindavinci.github.io/mdv-demo-arcade/games/tablature.html) — play the melody over a walking ground. *Tap on the beat.*
- [Portcullis](https://martindavinci.github.io/mdv-demo-arcade/games/portcullis.html) — stop what passes beneath the gatehouse. *Tap to drop the gate.*
- [Weft](https://martindavinci.github.io/mdv-demo-arcade/games/weft.html) — weave cloth that records every throw. *Tap to throw the shuttle.*
- [Pinion](https://martindavinci.github.io/mdv-demo-arcade/games/pinion.html) — keep falling feathers off the floor. *Tap to strike.*
- [Codex](https://martindavinci.github.io/mdv-demo-arcade/games/codex.html) — stack a library, one volume at a time. *Tap to drop the book.*

**How much** — the button is held, and the question is quantity.

- [Span](https://martindavinci.github.io/mdv-demo-arcade/games/span.html) — bridge the gap between towers at sunset. *Hold to grow, release to drop.*
- [Cornice](https://martindavinci.github.io/mdv-demo-arcade/games/cornice.html) — rooftops in the rain, at night. *Hold to charge, release to leap.*
- [Bellows](https://martindavinci.github.io/mdv-demo-arcade/games/bellows.html) — fly the channel between ranks of organ pipes. *Hold to rise.*
- [Ewer](https://martindavinci.github.io/mdv-demo-arcade/games/ewer.html) — fill jars to the mark as the belt carries them past. *Hold to pour.*
- [Aqueduct](https://martindavinci.github.io/mdv-demo-arcade/games/aqueduct.html) — swing down a sunlit aqueduct over open water. *Hold the ring, release at the top.*
- [Lintel](https://martindavinci.github.io/mdv-demo-arcade/games/lintel.html) — vault a bar that keeps rising. *Tap to plant, tap to release.*

**Which way** — the button makes a choice that can be wrong.

- [Chancery](https://martindavinci.github.io/mdv-demo-arcade/games/chancery.html) — every door wants its own wax. *Tap to change the seal.*
- [Ballast](https://martindavinci.github.io/mdv-demo-arcade/games/ballast.html) — sort cargo into a balance until it sits level. *Tap to throw the diverter.*
- [Transept](https://martindavinci.github.io/mdv-demo-arcade/games/transept.html) — two cats, mirrored across a cathedral floor. *One tap leaps both.*
- [Rotunda](https://martindavinci.github.io/mdv-demo-arcade/games/rotunda.html) — hold the centre of a circular library floor. *Tap to loose the shot.*

## Good to know

- **Scores are not saved.** Your best lasts until you reload the page.
- **Play with sound on.** Every effect is synthesised live, and several games tell you how well
  you are doing through pitch.
- **Each game is one HTML page** with its art and code inside. Once it has loaded it needs no
  network, and nothing is loaded from outside this site.
- **Chancery tells its wax seals apart by colour alone**, including a red and green pair that is
  hard to tell apart with red-green colour blindness.
